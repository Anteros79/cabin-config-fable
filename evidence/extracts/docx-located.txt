[P003] The measurements above are Southwest’s published ranges. Pitch is the distance from a point on one seat to the same point on the seat immediately ahead, not the amount of unobstructed knee clearance. [support.so...thwest.com]
[P004] {Heading 3} What distinguishes the four layouts
[P005] {list bullet L0} The 137-seat and 138-seat 737-700 layouts are both in circulation while 170 selected -700s are upgraded. The 138-seat version replaces Innovator seats with Meridian seats, adds in-seat power and converts the affected exit-row position to a triple-seat arrangement. Southwest expects the remaining non-upgraded aircraft to be retired, sold or otherwise removed from the fleet. Changes-and-Upcoming-Additions-to-Our-Fleet.aspx [Changes an...Our Fleet | SharePoint]
[P006] {list bullet L0} The 737-800 and MAX 8 temporary and final LOPAs both retain 175 total seats. The final layout adds one Extra Legroom seat on each side of the exit row, while the last row becomes two seats per side, keeping the overall capacity unchanged. Changes-and-Upcoming-Additions-to-Our-Fleet.aspx [Changes an...Our Fleet | SharePoint]
[P007] {list bullet L0} Existing aircraft can have Innovator, Collins Meridian or RECARO R2 seating, depending on the aircraft and retrofit status. New deliveries use the RECARO interior; selected -800s and -700s are also being converted as part of the standardization program. Revealed-Our-New-Elevated-Onboard-Experience.aspx [Revealed:...Experience | SharePoint]
[P008] {Heading 2} Customer-facing seat tiers
[P009] Southwest still operates one physical cabin, not a separate first- or business-class cabin. “Premium” refers to the Extra Legroom product within that cabin.

[P010] Southwest describes Extra Legroom as providing three to five additional inches of pitch: five inches on the 737-700 and three inches on the 737-800/MAX 8. Preferred is a location upgrade, not a dimensional upgrade. [support.so...thwest.com]
[P011] {Heading 3} Special 138-seat 737-700 treatment
[P012] On the upgraded 138-seat -700 layout, seats 7D, 7E, 7F, 15B and 15C physically have Extra Legroom spacing but are sold as Preferred, not Extra Legroom. That distinction needs to be retained in any configuration, seat-map or appearance-part application because the physical pitch and commercial product designation do not match.
